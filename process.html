How the process works

From first inquiry to close. Timing depends on financing and diligence.

Steps

#StepWhat happens
1InquiryYou send a short note: who you are, and whether you already operate.
2ScreenWBI confirms fit — operator experience, capital, and timeline.
3NDAConfidentiality before any detailed materials.
4CIMConfidential information memorandum and the financial package.
5CallIntro call, then an operations and equipment discussion.

8CloseDefinitive documents, funds, and training kickoff.
Typical clock from a serious inquiry: often about 4–6 months end to end. LOI to close is commonly 60–90 days when financing is clean.

What you’ll be asked for

  • Who you are, and whether you already operate in paving or sealcoating
  • Rough capital path — cash, SBA, bank, seller note, or a mix
  • Target close window
  • Proof of funds or a financing path later in the process, not on day one

What we will not do on a public page

  • Publish tax returns, the customer list, or equipment VINs
  • Promise SBA approval
  • Publish a phone number or the seller’s home address
