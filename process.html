How the process works

Simple path from first inquiry to close. Timeline varies with financing and diligence.

Steps

#StepWhat happens
1InquiryYou submit the form with background and buyer type.
2ScreenWBI confirms fit (operator / capital / timeline).
3NDATwo-way confidentiality before detailed materials.
4CIMConfidential Information Memorandum + high-level package.
5Call / visitIntro call, then equipment / operations discussion.
6LOIPrice, structure, financing, exclusivity, timeline.
7DiligenceTax returns, recon, titles, lease, vendors, etc.
8CloseDefinitive docs, funds, training kickoff.
Typical overall clock after market launch: often about 4–6 months end-to-end, with LOI→close commonly 60–90 days when financing is clean.

What you’ll be asked for

  • Who you are and whether you operate in paving/sealcoating already
  • Rough capital / financing path (cash, SBA, bank, seller note mix)
  • Target close window
  • Proof of funds or financing path later in process (not day one for all inquiries)

What we will not do on a public page

  • Publish tax returns, customer lists, or VINs
  • Promise SBA approval
  • Share seller personal home address
