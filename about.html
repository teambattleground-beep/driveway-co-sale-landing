About

Jeff bought The Driveway Company from a friend in 1999 after a layoff. He and Lisa have run it together since — field and estimates on one side, scheduling, payroll, and billing on the other.

We kept the original business phone. We don’t advertise. The book turns over because people expect us to call when the weather breaks.

How we’re set up

  • Ohio S-Corporation — legal name The Driveway Co.
  • Seasonal crews, not a year-round shop floor
  • Cash, check, or card (Square; 3% on cards)
  • 15 days to pay
